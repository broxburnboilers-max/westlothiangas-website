Questions agents ask us

Landlord gas safety, explained simply

Yes. UK law requires landlords to have every gas appliance and flue in a rental property checked annually by a Gas Safe registered engineer, with a valid CP12 certificate issued to the tenant within 28 days. West Lothian Gas carries out these checks and can put the renewal on a rolling annual plan so it's never missed.
For £29.99 a month on a 24-month term, the plan includes the annual gas safety certificate, an annual full boiler service, and breakdown cover for parts and labour on the boiler and heating system — all carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer with no extra call-out charges.
