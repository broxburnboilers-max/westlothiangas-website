A new boiler, fitted properly

Whether your old boiler has broken down for good or you simply want a more reliable, efficient system, West Lothian Gas can supply and install a new boiler with clear pricing before any work begins.

Every installation includes removing the existing boiler, fitting the new boiler and flue, a system filter, connecting to your existing system, new controls, and flushing the system and adding inhibitor on completion.

What's included

  • Remove the existing boiler
  • Supply and install a new condensing boiler complete with flue
  • Supply and install a system filter
  • Install new heating controls
  • Flush the system and add inhibitor on completion
  • Up to 10-year manufacturer warranty when serviced annually

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does a new boiler cost?

Our guide prices for a standard like-for-like Worcester combi installation are £2,300.60 (Worcester 1000 30kW), £2,567.40 (Worcester 2000 30kW) and £2,832.80 (Worcester 4000 30kW). Your final price is confirmed in writing after a free, no-obligation site survey.

Can I spread the cost of a new boiler?

Yes. Spread your installation cost over 12 equal monthly instalments at 0% interest on a simple signed agreement — no credit checks, no hidden fees, no interest added.

What warranty do I get?

Up to 10 years depending on the boiler. The warranty covers parts and labour for a repair to the boiler carried out by the manufacturer, provided the boiler is serviced once per year by a Gas Safe registered engineer.

What types of boiler do you install?

Combi, system and heat-only boilers, installed by a Gas Safe registered engineer to manufacturer instructions.

Can I view and sign my quote online?

Yes. View your boiler installation options, choose an option, sign electronically and download your copy.
