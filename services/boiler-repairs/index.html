No heating or hot water?

A boiler breakdown is stressful, especially in winter. West Lothian Gas offers fast local response across West Lothian and Edinburgh, with same day or next day appointments often available.

You get honest advice, practical options and a clear explanation of the problem before work begins — and if a repair isn't the most cost-effective option, we'll tell you.

Common faults we fix

  • No heating or no hot water
  • Leaking boilers and pipework
  • Low pressure and boilers locking out
  • Faulty parts, sensors and controls
  • Radiator and heating system issues

Emergencies

We offer emergency and out-of-hours call-outs. Call or WhatsApp 07961 768920 at any time. Emergencies are always our top priority. If you smell gas, call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 first.

Avoid repair bills

Our Service & Breakdown plan includes unlimited breakdown callouts with parts & labour included (up to £500/repair) and no excess fee per repair.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you come out?

Often same day or next day appointments are available, depending on availability. Emergencies are always our top priority.

Which boilers do you repair?

We diagnose and repair faults on most makes and models.

Will I know the cost before you start?

Yes. You get honest advice, practical options and a clear explanation of the problem before work begins, with clear pricing and no hidden fees.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 straight away, then contact us.

Can I get cover for future breakdowns?

Yes. Our Service & Breakdown plan (£19.99/month) includes unlimited breakdown callouts with no excess fee per repair. Compare plans.
