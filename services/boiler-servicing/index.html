Why service your boiler every year?

An annual service keeps your boiler safe, efficient and reliable, helps catch small problems before they become breakdowns, and is usually required to keep your manufacturer warranty valid.

What's included in a service

  • Full boiler service by a Gas Safe engineer
  • Gas safety check & flue inspection
  • Flue gas analysis where required
  • Boiler efficiency check & clean
  • Written service report provided
  • Advice on boiler health & lifespan

Prices

Annual boiler service: £80. Landlords can add the gas safety certificate (CP12) for a combined price of £110. No VAT to add.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How often should my boiler be serviced?

Once a year. Most manufacturer warranties require the boiler to be serviced once per year by a Gas Safe registered engineer.

What do I get after the service?

A written service report, plus advice on your boiler's health and lifespan.

Do you offer a service plan?

Yes. The Annual Service plan is £9.99/month on a 12-month minimum term. See the plan.
